    02

    Color

    One hue in OKLCH becomes a ten-step ramp. Contrast is computed live against the page, so every pairing is checked, not eyeballed. The hue also repaints this whole page.

      Ratios use the WCAG 2 luminance formula after an OKLCH to sRGB conversion. AA means 4.5:1 or better for body text, AA Large means 3:1 for 24px and up.

      03

      Motion

      Same distance, same duration, three curves. Ease-out does most of its moving up front, which is why interfaces that use it feel quick to respond.

      • linear
      • ease-in
      • cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)

      The button scales to 0.97 on press and springs back on release. Feedback you feel more than see.
